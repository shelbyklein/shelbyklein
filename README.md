# Shelby Klein

Designer and creative technologist in Atlanta, Georgia. I work across identity, digital products, creative tools, physical products, motion, and live experiences.

[Portfolio](https://shelbyklein.com) · [Writing](https://shelbyklein.com/writing/) · [Email](mailto:shelbykleindesign@gmail.com)

## What I make

I take ideas from concept through production: brand systems, websites, desktop applications, interactive tools, hardware, broadcasts, and the systems that hold creative work together.

I’m especially interested in human–AI interaction and in making AI useful around real projects. That includes fast, one-shot artifacts, long-running software development, and the many self-directed tasks that make up a creative practice.

## Current projects

- [Newton](https://shelbyklein.com/work/newton/) — a workspace for organizing AI teammates, projects, and recurring work.
- [Current](https://shelbyklein.com/work/current/) — a live rhyme studio for finding ideas while a line is still in motion.
- [PlayCase](https://shelbyklein.com/work/playcase/) — physical controls for playing classic games on an iPhone.

## Working with AI

I build and share practical ways to use AI around real creative work. The goal is simple: let the tools, project context, and way people need to execute work support one another.

I’m available for conversations and hands-on working sessions with creative teams exploring useful AI workflows.
