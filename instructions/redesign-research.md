## Cues

1. **Say who you are in one specific sentence.** The strongest openers name a person, a discipline, and a context: Maggie, Rauno, Brittany, Olivier. Slogans like "Ideas made tangible" could belong to anyone, and that was the core failure of attempt one.
2. **Commit to one signature idea, executed fully.** The most memorable sites each own one device: Dannaway's split face, Lynn's yearly edition, Bruno's car, Rauno's cursor object, Henry's computer. Everything around that device stays quiet.
3. **For a designer who builds, the site is the proof.** Craft shows up in the interaction, not in claims about it.
4. **Writing and teaching drive the traffic.** The highest-traffic personal sites (Emil, Josh, Lee, Maggie) are fueled by essays, tools, and courses, not galleries.
5. **Personal details build trust.** "Father to two radiant daughters" (Lee), "Korok seeds" (Brittany), "procrastiworking" (Jessica). Shelby's "designer, developer, dad" belongs in this tradition.
6. **Typography carries the identity.** The distinctive sites use a characterful face: Lynn's display, Maggie's serif, Olivier's serif and script mix, Tobias's condensed caps. Plain Geist plus grey was the second failure of attempt one.
7. **Show work right after the identity.** Dannaway and Noguier go straight to work: big titles, one image each, with clear hierarchy rather than a uniform grid.
