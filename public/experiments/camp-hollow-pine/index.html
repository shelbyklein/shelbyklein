Stop 01 · Cabin Row

Home base for the summer

Each camper lives in one of our twelve log cabins: eight bunks, two counselors, screened windows and a front porch made for rainy afternoons and card games.

  • Cabins are grouped by age, so first-timers are among friends
  • Nightly "cabin chat" before lights-out with your counselors
  • Letters home every Sunday, and we post photos daily

Stop 02 · The Lodge

Big tables, loud songs, seconds on pancakes

Meals are family-style, cabin by cabin. Our kitchen cooks from scratch and handles allergies and dietary needs with a separate prep line.

  • Upstairs: the arts loft for pottery, lanyards and woodburning
  • Mail call after lunch (yes, care packages are allowed)
  • Rainy days become talent shows and board-game tournaments

Stop 03 · The Tower

Climb it at your own pace

Our 40-foot climbing tower and the zipline down to the lake are the big moments of the summer. We run "challenge by choice": every step up counts, and so does the decision to stop.

  • Climbing, rappelling and the lakeside zipline
  • Archery range with certified instructors
  • Low ropes course for team challenges

Stop 04 · The Waterfront

Loon Lake, from first swim to sunset paddle

Every camper takes a swim check on day one, then picks from swimming, canoeing, kayaking, sailing and paddleboarding. Evenings end with the sun going down behind the far ridge.

  • Buddy system and tag board at every swim period
  • Overnight canoe trips for campers 13 and up
  • The Polar Bear Club: a 7am swim, every morning, if you dare
120acres of clear, spring-fed lake

Stop 05 · Campfire Circle

Every night ends here

Skits, stories and the same songs campers have sung here since 1952. Then flashlights off for five minutes of stars, a walk back to the cabins, and lights out.

  • S'mores on Fridays, plus the end-of-session candle boat ceremony
  • Stargazing nights with our astronomy counselor
  • Nurse on site around the clock in the health lodge
